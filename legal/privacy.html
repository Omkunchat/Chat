Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 2026

At Omkun Chat (Omkun Inc.), your privacy and data security are our top priorities. This policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your information.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Name, email, business details, and billing information.
  • API Data: WhatsApp Business API tokens required to facilitate messaging.
  • Customer Data: Chat logs, leads, and CRM data generated through your use of our platform.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data solely to provide, maintain, and improve our services. Chat logs and CRM data belong entirely to you. We process this data through AI (like Google Gemini) strictly to generate automated responses as configured by you.

3. Data Security & GDPR Compliance

All data transmitted between Omkun Chat, Meta, and Google is secured using industry-standard 256-bit encryption. We do not sell, rent, or trade your business data or your customers' data to any third parties.

4. Third-Party Services

To provide our services, we share necessary data with authorized third-party providers, including Meta Platforms Inc. (for WhatsApp API), Google (for AI processing), and our payment gateway partners.

For privacy concerns, contact: privacy@Omkun Chat.com
